Nevada rules

Which Nevada rules matter for Reno businesses?

General information only. Your tax preparer or attorney should confirm what applies to your business.

Taxes

Nevada has no state personal or corporate income tax. Reno businesses may owe the Modified Business Tax on wages, and businesses with Nevada gross revenue above a set level file the Commerce Tax. Clean payroll and revenue records make both easier.

Licenses

Reno businesses generally need a City of Reno business license. Contractors are licensed by the Nevada State Contractors Board, which sets a monetary limit on each license.

Law firm trust accounts

Reno lawyers keep client money under Rule 1.15 of the Nevada Rules of Professional Conduct. The lawyer stays responsible for the account, and Ray keeps the records that show every client’s money is accounted for.

Do Reno businesses pay state income tax?

Nevada has no state personal or corporate income tax. Reno businesses may still owe Nevada taxes such as the Modified Business Tax on wages and, above a set level of Nevada gross revenue, the Commerce Tax. Your tax preparer can confirm what applies to you.

Yes. A current WIP schedule tied to your books is usually the first thing a bank or surety asks for. Ray builds it, reviews the estimates behind it and explains it in plain words so you can answer their questions.
