Building in Truckee

Why is construction accounting different in Truckee?

Truckee sits in the Sierra Nevada in Nevada County, California, and snow shapes the building year.

Much of the site work in and around Truckee happens in the warmer months. Costs, billings and cash all bunch into that window, then slow down when the snow comes. For a builder, remodeler or trade contractor, that makes a monthly WIP schedule even more useful.

  • Fresh estimates to complete before the season closes, so a job does not fade over the winter
  • Over and under billings reviewed monthly, so cash for the slow months is planned
  • Job costs coded by job, so each project’s real profit is clear when it wraps up
  • Year-end schedules ready for your California tax preparer

California rules

Which California rules should a Truckee business keep in mind?

Truckee is a short drive from Nevada, but California rules apply. General information only. Confirm what applies to you with your tax preparer or attorney.

State taxes

California has a state income tax and a franchise tax that apply to many businesses. Nevada has neither, so the line between the 2 states matters.

Contractor licensing

Truckee contractors are licensed by the California Contractors State License Board, not the Nevada State Contractors Board.

Law firm trust accounts

California lawyers follow California Rule of Professional Conduct 1.15 and the State Bar’s Client Trust Account Protection Program, with yearly reporting.
