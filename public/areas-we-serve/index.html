Lake Tahoe, Nevada and California

How does Wilkerson Consulting serve the Lake Tahoe area?

Lake Tahoe is a region, not a single town. The lake sits across the Nevada and California state line, and the businesses around it answer to 2 different sets of state rules. Wilkerson Consulting serves the Lake Tahoe area on both sides.

On the Nevada side, that includes Incline Village and Crystal Bay in Washoe County, and Stateline and Zephyr Cove in Douglas County. On the California side, it includes South Lake Tahoe in El Dorado County, and Tahoe City and Kings Beach in Placer County.

Incline Village is about 35 miles from Reno over the Mount Rose Highway. South Lake Tahoe is about 60 miles away by way of Carson City and US 50. Most Lake Tahoe work happens by phone, video and shared files, and in-person meetings can be arranged.

2 states

Why does it matter which state your business is in?

Wilkerson Consulting works on both sides of the state line. General information only. Confirm what applies to you with your tax preparer or attorney.

Nevada

Reno, Carson City and the Nevada shore

Nevada has no state personal or corporate income tax. Businesses may owe the Modified Business Tax on wages and, above a set level of Nevada gross revenue, the Commerce Tax. Contractors are licensed by the Nevada State Contractors Board.

California

Truckee and the California shore

California has a state income tax and a franchise tax that apply to many businesses. Contractors are licensed by the Contractors State License Board, and law firms follow California’s own trust account rules and yearly reporting.

Is Lake Tahoe one town?

No. Lake Tahoe is a region that sits across the Nevada and California state line, with communities such as Incline Village and Stateline on the Nevada side and South Lake Tahoe and Tahoe City on the California side. Wilkerson Consulting serves the region on both sides.

Does Ray travel for meetings?

Ray can meet in person around Reno and Carson City. For Truckee and Lake Tahoe, phone and video meetings save the drive, and in-person meetings can be arranged.

What if my business is in Nevada but I work in California?

Tell Ray at the start. A Nevada business with jobs or clients in California, or the other way round, can face rules from both states, and the records need to show which work happened where.
