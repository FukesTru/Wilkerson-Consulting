Remote services

Far from Reno? The work still gets done.

Wilkerson Consulting is based in Reno, Nevada, and Ray Wilkerson, CPA, can work with your business by phone and video when you are not nearby.

Call (407) 488-7464 Contact us

Much of the work happens through shared files, and Ray meets in person around Reno and Carson City by arrangement.

How it works

How does remote work with Ray run?

Remote work with Wilkerson Consulting follows 3 steps, the same way it does for a business down the street from Ray in Reno.

  1. A first call

    You talk with Ray by phone or video about your business, your records and what you need. He explains the scope before any work starts.

  2. Share your files

    Ray tells you which records he needs and how to share them. Much of the work happens through shared files.

  3. Regular check-ins

    Ray keeps you posted as the work goes, with check-ins by phone or video.

Services

Which services can Ray provide remotely?

All 4 Wilkerson Consulting services work by phone, video and shared files.

  • Construction Accounting (WIP & Percentage of Completion)WIP schedules, percentage of completion and job cost reports for contractors.
  • Manufacturing Cost AccountingWhat each product costs to make, tied to your inventory.
  • Attorney Trust AccountingMonthly three-way reconciliations and cleanup for law firm trust accounts.
  • Forensic Accounting for Fraud or Profit/Loss AnalysisA careful look at your records when money or profit goes missing.
