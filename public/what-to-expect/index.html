Before you call

What should you have ready for the first call?

You do not need to prepare anything formal. These details help Ray understand your situation quickly.

  • For every businessWhat the business does, which accounting software you use, who keeps the books today, and any deadline from a bank, a bonding company, a partner or a tax date.
  • If you are a contractorAbout how many jobs are open, and your latest WIP schedule if you have one.
  • If you make productsWhat you make, whether you build to order or to stock, and how you track inventory today.
  • If you are a law firmWhich state bar you are licensed with and when the trust account was last reconciled.
  • If you suspect fraudContact Ray in a way the person you suspect will not see, and do not change or delete any records first.

Step by step

How does work with Wilkerson Consulting run from start to finish?

Every engagement at Wilkerson Consulting in Reno follows the same 5 steps, whether it is a one-time cleanup or a monthly report.

  1. First contact

    You call or text (407) 488-7464, email Ray or send the form. Ray gets back to you to set up a time to talk.

  2. A first conversation

    Ray asks about your business, your records and what you need. He tells you plainly whether the work is a good fit for Wilkerson Consulting.

  3. A look at the records, when needed

    Some work cannot be sized until Ray sees the records, such as a cleanup or a fraud review. In that case he looks first and scopes second.

  4. Scope agreed

    Ray explains what he will do and what he needs from you. Nothing starts until you agree.

  5. The work, with updates

    Ray keeps you posted as he goes. Monthly work, like WIP schedules or trust reconciliations, follows the same schedule every month.

Your people

Who will Ray work with at your business?

Ray works with the people who already keep your business running. That can be your bookkeeper, your office manager, your project managers or your shop supervisor. For outside advisers, it can be your tax preparer, your CPA firm or your attorney.

In Richmond, Virginia, Ray prepared the schedules a Richmond company’s outside CPA firm needed for its tax preparation work. A partner at that firm wrote that his work product was far better than what they had received in prior years.

Meetings

Can you meet Ray in person?

Ray is based in Reno and can meet in person around Reno and Carson City. For Truckee and the Lake Tahoe area, phone and video calls save the drive, especially in winter when Interstate 80 and the mountain passes can be slow.

Much of the work itself happens through shared files and access to your accounting software, so where you are matters less than you might think.

Questions and answers

Common questions about getting started

Ask Ray directly

Call or text (407) 488-7464 if you want to ask something else.

Call Ray Text Ray
Do I need to change my accounting software to work with Ray?

Ray starts from the software and records you already have. If a change would help, he will explain why and the choice stays with you.

What will Ray need access to?

Usually your accounting software, recent bank statements and the reports you already use. For construction work that includes job cost reports and contracts, and for trust work the trust bank statements and client ledgers. Ray tells you exactly what he needs for your work.

Can Ray work with my bookkeeper or office manager?

Yes. Ray works alongside whoever keeps your books today. In most cases the bookkeeper stays and Ray adds the reports, reconciliations and checks the business is missing.

What if my records are a mess?

That is one of the most common reasons people call. In Richmond, Virginia, Ray cleaned up the financial records of a 79-year-old family-run business. Cleanup starts with the most recent months and works back as far as you need.
