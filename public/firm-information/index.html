Wilkerson Consulting firm information

Wilkerson Consulting, LLC is an accounting and consulting firm based in Reno, Nevada. Its owner is Walter Raymond (Ray) Wilkerson, CPA. The firm offers construction accounting, manufacturing cost accounting, attorney trust accounting and forensic accounting to businesses and law firms in Reno, Carson City, Truckee and the Lake Tahoe area.

Services

What services does Wilkerson Consulting offer?

All services

Construction Accounting (WIP & Percentage of Completion)
Wilkerson Consulting prepares work in progress (WIP) schedules, percentage of completion reports and job cost reports for contractors.
Manufacturing Cost Accounting
Wilkerson Consulting works out what each product costs to make and ties those costs to inventory and the profit and loss statement.
Attorney Trust Accounting
Wilkerson Consulting keeps law firm trust account records and does monthly three-way reconciliations for firms in Nevada and California.
Forensic Accounting for Fraud or Profit/Loss Analysis
Wilkerson Consulting examines financial records when fraud is suspected or when profit has dropped without a clear reason.
