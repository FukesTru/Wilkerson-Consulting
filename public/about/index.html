About the owner

Ray Wilkerson, CPA, owner of Wilkerson Consulting in Reno

Wilkerson Consulting is an accounting and consulting firm in Reno, Nevada, owned by Walter Raymond (Ray) Wilkerson, CPA. Ray was well established and known in Richmond, Virginia for more than 30 years. He handles construction, manufacturing cost, attorney trust and forensic accounting for businesses in Reno, Carson City, Truckee and Lake Tahoe.

Public accounting

Where did Ray’s career in accounting begin?

Ray had his own public accounting firm before the move to the business side.

In 1994 Ray merged his public accounting firm with a larger regional firm and retired from public accounting, taking a 10-year buy-out.

A buy-out is an agreement in which the firm that takes over pays the former owner for his share over a set period. Ray’s ran for 10 years.

Ray Wilkerson is a CPA, Virginia license #5165.

Running a business

What did Ray do after public accounting?

He ran a business of his own for 20 years.

In 1998 Ray, his wife, his son and his son’s wife started a commercial cleaning business. Over the next 20 years Ray grew it into a specialty decorative and polished concrete installation, restoration and maintenance business with sales of $2.5 to $3.0 million a year. His son and daughter-in-law then took the business over.

That meant 20 years of payroll, customer invoices, crews and cash flow, seen from the owner’s side of the desk.

Contract and Fractional CFO work

What has Ray done since he came back to accounting?

After the family business passed to his son and daughter-in-law, Ray became active in accounting again.

Ray has done short-term contract work and Fractional CFO and controller work for many established companies in different industries. A Fractional CFO or controller is a senior finance person who works part time for a company that does not need one full time.

For about the last 2 years, according to a partner at Brown Edwards, Ray worked as Fractional CFO and controller for W. W. Nash & Sons, Inc. and affiliated companies in Richmond, Virginia. The letters from that company and from its outside CPA firm describe the work.

What the letters describe

Ray’s work in Richmond, Virginia

  • Cleaning up the financial records of a 79-year-old family-run business.
  • Helping create and refine the company’s WIP process. WIP stands for work in progress, the report a contractor uses to track every open job.
  • Setting up audit procedures and internal processes.
  • Getting the last 2 year-end closings done sooner.
  • Preparing the schedules the outside CPA firm needed for its tax preparation work.
