What drives the cost

What decides the cost of accounting work in Reno?

5 things decide how much time the work takes, and so what it costs.

  • How much there is to trackThe number of open jobs, products, bank accounts or client ledgers.
  • How current the records areA business that is months behind needs catching up before monthly work can start.
  • What the work is forA bank or bonding company request, a year-end close, a dispute or your own decisions.
  • One time or every monthA single project or a report you need each month.
  • Your deadlineA tight date from a lender, a surety or a tax filing can change how the work is planned.

Kinds of work

Which work is a one-time project and which is monthly?

Most work at Wilkerson Consulting falls into one of these 2 kinds. Many clients start with a project and then move to monthly work.

One-time projects

A piece of work with a clear finish

  • Cleaning up past months or years of records
  • A forensic review when you suspect fraud or a profit drop
  • Setting up a WIP process for a contractor
  • Building a product costing method for a manufacturer
  • Bringing a law firm trust account back into balance

Monthly work

The same reports, every month

  • WIP schedules and job cost reports for contractors
  • Product cost and inventory reports for manufacturers
  • Three-way trust account reconciliations for law firms
  • Year-end schedules for your outside tax preparer

Questions and answers

Questions about fees

Not covered here? Ask Ray directly.

Does Wilkerson Consulting publish a price list?

No. Every business, job list and trust account is different, so a list price would be wrong for most people. Ray discusses your fee with you up front instead.

Do you charge by the hour or a set fee?

It depends on the work. Ray explains how your work would be billed before it starts, so you can decide with the full picture.

Can I pay on this website?

No. Nothing is ever paid on this website. Ray explains billing and payment when you agree on the work.

Why does it matter how current my records are?

Bringing past months up to date takes more time than keeping the current month in order. Records that are already current usually mean a smaller first piece of work.
