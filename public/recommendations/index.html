Recommendations for Ray Wilkerson, CPA

Ray Wilkerson, CPA, owner of Wilkerson Consulting in Reno, Nevada, has 2 signed letters of recommendation. One is from Adam Hoover, Vice President of W.W. Nash and Sons Inc. in Richmond, Virginia. The other is from Jacob D. Favaro, CPA, CFP®, a Partner at Brown Edwards, the CPA firm that provides that company’s tax preparation services.

The letters

What do the letters say, in their own words?

These are excerpts, quoted word for word, from 2 signed letters of recommendation.

Adam Hoover

Vice President, W.W. Nash and Sons Inc. Letter dated June 19, 2025.

“Ray has been an invaluable member of our team for the past fourteen months, and his contributions have significantly elevated our financial operations and internal controls.”

“He was instrumental in undertaking the monumental task of meticulously cleaning up the financial records of a 79-year-old family-run business.”

“This project required a deep understanding of historical transactions, keen investigative skills, and an unwavering commitment to accuracy, all of which Ray executed with remarkable success.”

“He was fundamental in assisting with the creation and refinement of our WIP processes, developing a system that I confidently believe would rival those found in construction companies ten times our size.”

“Furthermore, Ray took the initiative to establish robust audit procedures and internal processes, enhancing our overall accounting accuracy and compliance.”

Jacob D. Favaro, CPA, CFP®

Partner, Brown Edwards. Letter dated July 30, 2026.

“For approximately the last two years, Ray has worked as Fractional CFO/Controller for one of my clients, W. W. Nash & Sons, Inc. and affiliated companies in Richmond, VA.”

“He has improved the timing of their year-end closings for the last two year-ends and prepared the schedules our team has needed for our Tax Preparation Services.”

“Our team and our client have found his work product to be far superior to what we received in prior years.”

“He has a strong knowledge of accounting principles and taxation, especially with the construction industry.”

Mr. Favaro’s letter also notes that Ray is a member of the Construction Financial Managers Association.

Excerpts from signed letters of recommendation. Every business is different, and these letters do not promise the same result for yours.
